Lumen Sort privacy policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026. Privacy contact: ugevidevelopment@gmail.com.

Game progress and preferences

The game stores your level, board, move history, help credits and limits, ad milestones, tutorial status, and sound and vibration preferences on your device. It keeps a local recovery copy. The game does not require an account or send game saves to a developer server. Android backups and device transfers may depend on your settings.

Permissions and connectivity

The game declares vibration permission for tactile feedback. Advertising components use internet access and may add permissions such as network status and advertising ID access. The reviewed game code does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts or precise location.

Google advertising

Lumen Sort integrates Google AdMob for banners, interstitials between levels and optional rewarded ads. Google states that its SDK collects and shares IP addresses, product interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. This may include the advertising ID, depending on the device and configuration. The game does not include the board or move history in ad requests. QA and development builds use test ad units when ads are enabled. Commercial advertising is disabled in the reviewed code. Test builds may use AdMob and UMP with test ad units. The game requests non-personalized ads for all three formats. This setting does not prevent the SDK from processing data for other purposes, such as measurement and fraud prevention.

Consent and choices

Lumen Sort uses Google UMP to update consent status and show forms where applicable. The game checks UMP before requesting ads. When UMP requires it, you can review your choices under Settings → Ad privacy. Android and Google offer additional controls for advertising and personalization.

Teen audience

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older. The target audience declared in Google Play does not establish an individual player’s age or determine which ads they receive. The game does not ask for age or send an age signal to UMP. In test builds, it requests teen ad treatment and a maximum PG ad content rating from Google Mobile Ads for all requests. These settings do not identify the player’s age. The reviewed version does not request commercial ads for any player, including teens and users of unknown age. Commercial ad serving will be evaluated by market before it is enabled. Consent and protection requirements vary by jurisdiction.

Retention, deletion and rights

Game progress is stored on your device. You can remove local data through Android’s app storage settings, subject to any backups you have configured. See Google’s privacy policy for its retention practices and deletion options. For applicable privacy rights requests or questions, email ugevidevelopment@gmail.com.
