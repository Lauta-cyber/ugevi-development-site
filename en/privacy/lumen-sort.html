Lumen Sort privacy policy

Who is responsible

Lumen Sort for Android (com.ugevi.lumensort) is developed under the UGeVi Development brand in Argentina. Legal data controller: LAUTARO EZEQUIEL URQUIZA.
Address: Mza 228 CS 02 B° Cerro De La Cruz San Luis - D5700 Argentina (AR).
Country: Argentina.
Effective date: November 10, 2026. Privacy contact: ugevidevelopment@gmail.com.

Game progress and preferences

Lumen Sort stores game progress, the board, move history, and preferences on the device. It also stores a local recovery copy. The game does not require an account or send that data to UGeVi Development. If backups are enabled, Android may include game preferences, including progress, in a backup associated with the user’s account or transfer them to another device, depending on Android and account settings. UGeVi Development does not receive or manage those backups.

Permissions and connectivity

Production version 1.0.0 requests the vibration permission for haptic feedback. It does not request the Internet permission or make its own network connections. Opening the privacy policy from the game launches an external browser.

Advertising and data collection

Production version 1.0.0 does not show ads or include Google AdMob, Google UMP, or analytics SDKs. The game does not collect or share user data in this version.

Teen audience

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. This version does not ask for age or show advertising.

Retention, deletion and rights

You can erase game progress, preferences, and the local recovery copy through Android’s storage settings. To manage Android backups, see the backup options for your device and account. To exercise applicable rights or ask a privacy question, email ugevidevelopment@gmail.com.
