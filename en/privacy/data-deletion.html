LUMEN SORT / ANDROID

Lumen Sort data deletion

You can request deletion of personal data controlled by UGeVi Development by email. Lumen Sort does not require a player account.

How to submit a request

  1. Email ugevidevelopment@gmail.com and mention Lumen Sort in the subject.
  2. Tell us what data you want deleted. For a message you sent us, reply in the same thread or provide its subject and approximate date. Keep passwords and identity documents out of your initial message.
  3. We will review what data we control. If we need to clarify or verify the request, we will reply by email. We will explain what we can delete and whether an applicable obligation limits deletion.

Data UGeVi Development can manage

A request may cover personal data you sent directly to us, such as your email address and support message contents, where we control that data and deletion is applicable. If we cannot locate the data from your request, we may ask for the minimum information needed to find it.

Game progress and preferences on your device

Game progress, preferences and the game’s recovery copy are stored locally. UGeVi Development cannot erase them remotely. To delete them, open Android Settings, find Lumen Sort and choose Storage → Clear data or Clear storage. This resets game progress and preferences. Android-managed backups depend on your device’s backup settings.

Data managed by Google

Google may process data through AdMob and UMP when those services are active. UGeVi Development cannot directly delete data managed by Google. See Google’s privacy and deletion options. Changing ad consent does not request deletion of data already processed.
